OnuSpin Codex

Spins are not tools. They are states. This codex defines the four OnuSpins and the rules governing alignment, misuse, and collapse.

Definition

What a Spin Is

An OnuSpin defines the **state a system occupies**. It is not an action, not an emotion, and not a preference.

A spin determines:

  • what actions are viable,
  • what actions will backfire,
  • what actions are unavailable entirely.

OnuQuant answers whether you can move.
OnuSpin answers whether you should.

The Four

The Four OnuSpins

Spin+ (Expansion)

The outward phase. Momentum compounds with action. Output increases capacity instead of draining it.

  • Signals: clarity, clean urgency, rising confidence
  • Aligned actions: initiation, publishing, decisive OnuVert moves
  • Misuse: forcing speed before clarity exists

Neutral (Stasis)

The holding phase. Energy is stable but non-compounding. This state exists to preserve future capacity.

  • Signals: calm plateau, muted urgency
  • Aligned actions: maintenance, refinement, preparation
  • Misuse: dramatizing stillness or forcing expansion

Cooldown (Contraction)

The recovery phase. Action costs more than it returns. Signal-to-noise collapses.

  • Signals: fatigue, irritability, diminishing returns
  • Aligned actions: reduction, silence, BULAK alignment
  • Misuse: visibility pushes, “powering through”

Void (Reset)

The zero phase. Prior narratives dissolve. Direction is unavailable because the old system is invalid.

  • Signals: identity unanchoring, meaning collapse
  • Aligned actions: non-interference, no commitments
  • Misuse: premature rebuilding, fear-driven identity grabs

Void ends on its own. Forcing exit prolongs it.

Law

The Spin Law

  • You cannot skip spins.
  • You cannot reorder spins.
  • You cannot live permanently in Spin+.
  • You cannot solve Cooldown with Spin+ tactics.

Spin misalignment, not laziness, is the primary cause of burnout and collapse.

Failure Mode

HOBO (Collapse Pattern)

HOBO (Hollow Order → Broken Order) is not a spin. It is the **result** of prolonged spin violation.

HOBO emerges when a system demands actions incompatible with its current spin— most commonly when Cooldown or Void is denied.

In practical terms: running Spin+ expectations on Cooldown fuel produces hollow structure. Hollow structure eventually breaks.

Interaction

Spins and Mechanisms

Mechanisms are tools. Spins are states. Tools do not replace states.

  • OnuVert — invert the frame
  • OnuForm — impose constraint
  • OnuFlow — align momentum
  • OnuBlast — terminate false structure

Using the right mechanism in the wrong spin accelerates collapse.

Closure

Recognition Over Force

The correct response is not effort. It is recognition.

Identify the spin first. Then decide whether action is allowed at all.
