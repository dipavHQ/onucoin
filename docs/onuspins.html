OnuSpin Codex

Spins are states you inhabit, not tools you use. This page defines the four spins, the law that governs them, and how they interact with OnuQuant and the mechanisms.

1 · Definition

What a Spin Is

OnuSpin defines the state your system occupies. A spin is not an action, not a mood, and not a personality. It is the configuration of your available energy, attention, and narrative stability.

Key distinction

  • OnuQuant = fuel (how much activation is available)
  • OnuSpin = state (how that activation behaves over time)

Most failure is not lack of effort. It is spin misalignment: attempting Spin+ actions in Cooldown, or forcing decisions inside Void.

2 · The Four

The Four Spins

Spin+ · Expansion

The outward phase. Momentum compounds with action. Output increases capacity rather than draining it.

  • Signals: clarity rises, action feels clean, decisions speed up
  • Correct moves: initiate, publish, build, ship, OnuVert with precision
  • Incorrect moves: over-analysis, unnecessary withdrawal, premature collapse rituals

Neutral · Stasis

The holding phase. Movement is possible, but non-compounding. This state exists to preserve capacity and prevent unnecessary depletion.

  • Signals: stable energy, muted urgency, progress is slow but possible
  • Correct moves: maintain, refine, observe, prepare, tighten boundaries
  • Incorrect moves: forcing expansion, self-criticism for plateau, dramatizing stillness

Cooldown · Contraction

The recovery phase. Action costs more than it returns. Stimulation overwhelms. Signal-to-noise collapses.

  • Signals: fatigue, irritability, diminished returns, sensory overload
  • Correct moves: reduce, restore, disengage, silence, BULAK alignment
  • Incorrect moves: visibility pushes, urgency theater, “powering through,” forced optimism

Void · Reset

The zero phase. Prior narratives dissolve and new ones are not yet legible. Direction is unavailable because the old system is no longer valid.

  • Signals: identity unanchoring, meaning collapse, inability to choose direction
  • Correct moves: non-interference, no commitments, no identity grabs
  • Incorrect moves: reattachment, premature rebuilding, choosing from fear

Void ends on its own. Forcing exit prolongs it.

3 · Law

The Spin Law

  • You cannot skip spins.
  • You cannot reorder spins.
  • You cannot live permanently in Spin+.
  • You cannot solve Cooldown with Spin+ tactics.

A spin is not a preference. It is a state. The correct approach is recognition, not force.

4 · Interaction

Spins vs Mechanisms

Mechanisms are discrete tools. Spins are persistent states. You use mechanisms inside a spin; you do not replace the spin with the mechanism.

Mechanisms (tools)

  • OnuVert — invert meaning (reframe the axis, not the answer)
  • OnuForm — structure (constraints that force clarity)
  • OnuFlow — integrate (alignment over force)
  • OnuBlast — terminate (end the loop decisively)

Operating rule

Identify the spin first. Then decide whether action is allowed at all. Wrong-tool / wrong-spin usage creates friction, drains OnuQuant, and accelerates collapse.

Canon line: OnuQuant answers whether you can move. OnuSpin answers whether you should.
