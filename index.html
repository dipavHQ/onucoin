Why “OnuCoin” Exists at All

OnuCoin starts with a reversal. The name comes from taking UNO — the game of chaotic turns and surprise reversals — and flipping it to ONU.

UNO is about disruption, unexpected direction changes, and the sheer satisfaction of disrupting someone else’s perfect little plan. When you reverse UNO, you get the meta-move:

OnuCoin is that reversal turned into a token. A signal for people who quietly decide: “No. It doesn’t go that way anymore.”
