Tokenomics

OnuCoin (ONU) is designed with deliberate restraint. This page explains the structure — and the limits — of the system.

1 · Supply

Fixed, Four-Coded Supply

ONU has a fixed total supply of 4,444,444. This number is structural, not aesthetic.

  • Four pillars of DIPAV
  • Four phases of reversal
  • Fourfold cosmology (Draw Four)

There is no inflation schedule. No minting function. No algorithmic expansion.

2 · Distribution

Distribution Philosophy

DIPAV does not optimize for speed. Distribution is intentionally conservative and narrative-led.

Current State

  • No public sale
  • No liquidity pool
  • No emissions schedule
  • No yield farming

The token exists before the market does. This is deliberate.

Any future distribution events, if they occur, will be explicit, documented, and bounded by DIPAV constraints.

3 · Liquidity

Liquidity as a Story Beat

ONU rejects the default assumption that liquidity must exist immediately.

  • Liquidity is optional, not mandatory
  • Markets are treated as chapters, not foundations
  • Silence is part of the signal

If liquidity is ever introduced, it will be:

  • announced intentionally
  • framed as a narrative event
  • bounded by explicit constraints
  • aligned with DIPAV doctrine
4 · Constraints

Anti-Vapid Constraints

To prevent ONU from degrading into speculation theater, the following constraints apply:

No Hype Incentives

No staking rewards. No APY bait. No gamified extraction.

No Forced Velocity

The token does not require movement to be valid. Holding is not framed as failure.

No Performance Promises

ONU does not promise appreciation. It does not signal returns.

Culture as the Anchor

The value proposition is cultural coherence. Markets, if present, are secondary.

Closing

The Point of Restraint

Most tokens optimize for attention. ONU optimizes for meaning.

This page is intentionally boring. That is the design.

If you are looking for velocity, hype, or spectacle, this system will disappoint you.

If you are looking for coherence, restraint, and symbolic clarity, you are in the right place.
